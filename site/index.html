哲学 · philosophy

What is hansei?

反省 — hansei — is the Japanese practice of honest self-reflection. After every effort, you pause: what worked, what didn’t, what could be better — even when everything went right.

We build that pause into the way we design. Every screen, system and interaction ships only after it has been questioned, stripped back and refined. The result is work that feels calm, intentional and effortless — because everything unnecessary has been reflected away.

“Design is not done when there is nothing left to add,
but when there is nothing left to question.”

Process

  1. 観

    Observe

    We start by listening — to you, your users and your constraints. No pixels until the problem is sharp.

  2. 作

    Craft

    Design and build move together in short, visible loops — you see real progress, not decks.

  3. 省

    Reflect

    The hansei step. We critique our own work harder than anyone else will, and cut what doesn’t earn its place.

  4. 磨

    Polish

    Motion, accessibility, performance, detail. The last 10% is the part people feel but never notice.
